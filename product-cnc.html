CNC Turning

CNC Lathes

High-rigidity slant-bed turning centers engineered for shafts, discs and complex parts — stable cutting, fast turret indexing, ready for automation.

Designed for Production Turning

The Yusheng CNC lathe range combines a rigid cast-iron slant bed, a fast-response spindle and an interference-free turret zone, so you can push material-removal rates without losing the surface finish or tolerances that make parts good.

  • 30° slant cast-iron bed for rigidity and excellent chip flow
  • Servo turret with fast, bidirectional indexing
  • Optional C-axis and live tooling for milling on turned parts
  • Ready for bar feeder, gantry loader or robot integration
  • Fully enclosed guarding with automatic chip conveyor

Robots & Manipulators

Articulated and gantry robots that load machines, move parts and palletize output — flexible enough to retool for the next part.

The Muscle Behind Flexible Cells

A robot makes a line flexible: the same station runs a new part tomorrow because the robot's program changes, not its hardware. Yusheng supplies articulated 6-axis robots and gantry manipulators, engineered with grippers, vision and safety, and wired into your machine cycle.

  • 6-axis articulated robots for complex paths and tight spaces
  • Gantry manipulators for long-stroke, heavy payloads
  • Custom grippers, chucks and vision options
  • Integrated safety — fences, light curtains, interlocks
  • Single supplier for machine + robot + loader

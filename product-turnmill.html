Multi-Axis Turn-Mill

Turn-Mill Centers

Milling, drilling and turning in one clamping — C-axis, Y-axis and live tooling turn complex parts into a single set-up.

One Clamping, Complete Machining

Transferring a part between machines costs time, fixtures, and — most importantly — accuracy. Yusheng turn-mill centers keep the workpiece in one clamping while turning, contour milling, drilling, tapping and even off-axis machining happen in a single program.

  • C-axis for contouring and positioning work
  • Optional Y-axis for true off-center milling
  • High-torque BMT live-tool turret
  • Optional sub-spindle for back-side machining
  • Saves fixtures, transfers and total cycle time

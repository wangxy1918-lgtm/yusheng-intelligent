Auto Load/Unload Systems

Gantry loaders, pallet changers and bar feeders that keep your machines cutting — through breaks, nights and weekends.

Make the Lights-Out Shift Real

A machine that waits for an operator is a machine that doesn't earn. Yusheng load/unload systems hand parts to your CNC machines automatically — so utilization climbs, labor costs drop, and quality stays consistent because every cycle is identical.

  • Gantry loaders built to match your machine and part
  • Pallet changers and multi-fixture tables for longer runs
  • Bar feeders for unattended long-run turning
  • Integrated into the machine control — no extra operator
  • Safety guarding and interlocks as standard

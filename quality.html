Precision is a process

Quality & Precision

A machine's accuracy on your shop floor starts long before delivery. Every Yusheng machine is checked, run-in and documented before it ships.

Incoming Parts & Components

Castings, spindles, guideways and ball screws are inspected before assembly — we don't assemble problems we could have caught earlier.

Assembly & Alignment

Geometric alignment of the spindle, axes and turret to tight reference values, checked with precision instruments.

Run-in & Cutting Test

Every machine is run continuously, then cutting tests confirm the promised accuracy — before packaging, not after installation.

Inspection Report & Acceptance

Geometric and test results are recorded in an inspection report shipped with the machine — you sign off on what we measured.

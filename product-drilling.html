Drilling & Tapping Centers

High-speed spindles, fast tool changes and rigid tapping built for batch production — where cycle time is the whole game.

Cycle Time Is the Product

For connectors, housings, brackets and thousands of small drilled parts, the winner is whoever machines fastest without scrapping parts. Yusheng drilling & tapping centers pair a high-speed spindle with fast rapids and a quick tool changer, so every second goes into the part — not the machine.

  • High-speed spindle for drilling and light milling
  • Fast tool change and rapid traverse for tight cycles
  • Rigid tapping as standard — clean threads at speed
  • Compact footprint that fits more machines per square meter
  • Ready to pair with loader or robot for unmanned runs

Application 01

Automotive Parts

Axles, gears, hubs and brake components run in big batches with zero tolerance for drift. The machine has to hold geometry shift after shift — and keep up with demand.

  • Rigid slant-bed lathes for stable heavy-removal turning
  • Bar feeders and gantry loaders for continuous batch runs
  • Automation options that cut labor while raising output
Application 02

3C Electronics

Connectors, housings and thousands of small drilled parts demand speed: short cycles, fast tool changes and quick changeover between models.

  • High-speed drilling & tapping centers built for cycle time
  • Compact footprint to fit more machines per square meter
  • Robot pairing for automatic load/unload of small parts
Application 03

Mould & Hardware

Mould cores, inserts and hardware blanks need rigidity and surface quality — chatter is the enemy, and finish is the requirement.

  • High-rigidity structures for chatter-free finishing
  • Turn-mill capability for complete mould-core machining
  • Process support on speeds, feeds and tooling
Application 04

Aerospace

Light-alloy components with traceable, repeatable machining and complete documentation. Every part is a record; every machine must be reliable enough to trust.

  • Stable, repeatable machines for documented processes
  • Precision turning of light-alloy shafts and housings
  • Qualification and run-in documentation on every machine
Application 05

Medical Devices

Instrument parts must be clean, precise and fully traceable. Machines run small exotic batches; changeover speed and repeatability decide the economics.

  • Precise turning and drilling of instrument components
  • Fast changeover for small, mixed batches
  • Material and process traceability support

About Yusheng

A Zhongshan-based team that designs and builds precision machining equipment — from standard CNC lathes to fully custom automated production lines.

Our story

Machinery People, Machining People

Yusheng was founded in the machine-building hub of Zhongshan, Guangdong — a city where thousands of parts are machined every day. Our engineers came from the shop floor, not just the drawing board. They have run lathes, dialed in processes and solved real production problems.

That background shapes everything we build. Machines are designed to be practical on the floor, easy for operators, and dependable year after year. When a customer has a part that needs a different approach, we engineer a machine around it rather than pushing them toward a catalog.

Custom Machining Solutions

Off-the-shelf machines can't do everything. When a standard machine won't hit your cycle time, tolerance or budget — we engineer the machine and the automation around your part.

How we work

From Drawing to Running Line

One engineering team owns the whole project — so the machine, the automation and the process fit together the first time.

Send Your Part

Drawings, a sample part or just a photo of the finished piece — plus target quantity and cycle time.

Process & Machine Proposal

Our engineers design the machining process, select or design the machine, and quote the complete solution.

Build, Trial & Acceptance

We build, trial-run with your actual parts, and agree acceptance criteria before the machine ships.

Install, Train & Support

Commissioning at your plant, operator training, and ongoing spare-parts and service support.

What we can build

Solution Scope

Special-Purpose Machines

Machines designed for one part family — combining turning, drilling, tapping and milling into a single automated cell.

Automation Cells

Gantry loaders, robots and bar feeders that feed, unload and stack — letting one operator run several machines.

Turnkey Production Lines

Multiple machines, connected material handling and process control — a complete line for a complete part.

The payoff

Why a Custom Solution Wins

A purpose-built machine doesn't have to cost more than a standard one with expensive add-ons. It just has to fit.

  • Cycle time designed for your part, not averaged over many
  • Fewer setups — more operations completed in one clamping
  • Automation matched to the part, not bolted on
  • One accountable engineering team from quote to handover
